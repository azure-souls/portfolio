Intelligent Multi-Window Switching System

2024

Overview

Developed on the STM32F103C8T6 platform, achieving synchronized dual DC brushed motor control via Hall encoders. Designed a Zigbee wireless communication module based on the CC2530 chip and established a mesh network to enable distributed motor control, realizing centralized switching of windows across multiple walls in a multi-workshop environment.
